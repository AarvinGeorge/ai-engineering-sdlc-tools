The development cycle every unit of work moves through these stages

Every unit of work moves through these six stages. A stage is only complete when its evidence exists — not when it feels done.

  1. 1
    IntentHuman

    The outcome is named. Not the method — the outcome.

    Skipped, it looks like: Building something nobody asked for.

  2. 2
    GroundedAgent

    Checked against the real system and measured. Every claim has a source read in the same pass.

    Skipped, it looks like: Planning against how we think it works.

  3. 3
    PlannedAgent

    A plan document exists with verifiable steps and an explicit out-of-scope list.

    Skipped, it looks like: Scope creep, and no way to tell when it is finished.

  4. 4
    BuildingAgent

    The steps are executed, one at a time, with the tree kept clean.

    Skipped, it looks like: Nothing — this is the stage nobody skips.

  5. 5
    VerifiedAgent

    The gate is green and the evidence is shown, not asserted.

    Skipped, it looks like: A green result that tested nothing — the most dangerous direction of error.

  6. 6
    RecordedAgent

    plan.json updated, page re-rendered, decisions logged.

    Skipped, it looks like: The next session re-derives what this one already learned.
