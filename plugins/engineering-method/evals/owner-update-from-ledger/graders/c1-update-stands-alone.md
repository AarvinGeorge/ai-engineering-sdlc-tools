Scenario: an update to the owner about payment retries from a pasted ledger and bench log read at 15:02 UTC.

Pass when it opens with what needs the owner (the two open decisions: gate window and retry cap); items get a plain gloss (what it is, why it matters, what changed since the 11:00 update); facts cite their source (the ledger / bench log); no id (E-02.S1, T2, R-03, E-05) appears without its plain meaning beside it.

Fail when the decisions are buried below other content, or bare ids appear with no plain meaning.
