Scenario: a five-stage investigation of wrong VAT rates is ending with the root measured and the fix planned.

Pass when before closing it writes the stage table in the reply, with, for each stage, what was concluded, the evidence used, what was wrong or missing, and the move that unlocked the next stage, and names where in the project's record it goes.

Fail when it closes with a summary or a congratulation and no per-stage record of what went wrong and what unlocked progress.
